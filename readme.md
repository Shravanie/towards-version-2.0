# Towards Version 2.0

## Project Summary

Towards Version 2.0 is a web-based student growth and progress tracking platform developed using Python, Flask, MySQL, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

The platform is designed to help students manage their personal and academic growth by creating activities, tracking their daily progress, maintaining activity history, and analyzing their performance over time. Students can monitor important metrics such as completion rate, current streak, best streak, and total time invested. The platform also provides weekly, monthly, and activity-wise progress through interactive charts.

The system has two types of users: **Students** and **Mentors**. Students can create and manage their activities, record their daily progress, and view their growth analytics. Mentors can view their assigned students, monitor their activities and performance, and provide feedback. Mentors can also edit or delete previously given feedback.

The application uses Flask as the backend framework, MySQL for data storage, Jinja templates for dynamic HTML pages, and Chart.js for data visualization. The project follows a simple modular structure to keep the application organized, maintainable, and suitable for a college-level full-stack project.

### Main Objectives

- Help students organize and track their growth activities.
- Allow students to record and review their daily progress.
- Calculate meaningful progress statistics such as completion rates and streaks.
- Visualize student growth using charts and analytics.
- Allow mentors to monitor assigned students.
- Enable mentors to provide constructive feedback.
- Provide a simple and user-friendly platform for continuous student development.
